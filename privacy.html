Privacy Policy

Lalitha Order Bot (Demo) is a demonstration WhatsApp ordering assistant for a small home food business. This policy explains what information the bot handles and why.

What we collect

  • Your WhatsApp phone number, so the bot can reply to you and associate your order with you.
  • The messages you send to the bot (for example, your food order), so your order can be prepared.
  • Your WhatsApp profile name, if WhatsApp shares it with the message.

How it is used

Solely to take, confirm, change, or cancel your food order and to show the order to the business owner on a private dashboard. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with third parties except WhatsApp/Meta, which carries the messages.

Retention

In this demo, orders are kept only in the server's temporary memory and are discarded when the service restarts. In a production version, order records would be kept only as long as needed to fulfil and account for orders.

Your choices

You may stop messaging the bot at any time. To ask for your information to be deleted, contact us at the email below.
